Calibrate

Do this once for each position you strap the ring to (wrist, back of hand, finger…). It teaches the system which way is "right" and "up" for that position. Takes about 30 seconds.

Step 1: Put the ring down and don't touch it

Lay it on the table. This measures the sensor's tiny built-in drift.

Step 2: Wear it and hold your normal pointing pose

Strap it on, point at the screen as you would to use it, and hold still.

Step 3: Turn your hand to the right

Click, then smoothly turn your hand right by about a quarter turn and stop. You have 3 seconds.

Step 4: Tip your hand up

Click, then tip your hand up (point higher) by about a quarter turn and stop.

Step 5: Save

Try moving the cursor. If right is right and up is up, save it to the device.

Check your computer's mouse acceleration

Your computer can add its own "mouse acceleration" on top of ours, which changes how far the cursor goes depending on speed. Turn it off for fair tests: on Windows, untick Enhance pointer precision (Settings → Mouse → Additional mouse settings → Pointer Options). This check tells you whether it's on.

Pick what you're testing in the Testing menu at the top first. Every result is saved under that name.

A. Lag test

Film your hand and this black box together with your phone's slow-motion (240 fps) camera. Hold still for a moment, then flick your hand. The box flashes white the instant the cursor starts moving. In the video, count frames from "hand starts moving" to "box turns white". Each frame is about 4.2 ms. Do 5 flicks, then type your frame counts below.

B. Steadiness test

Point at the cross, click Start, and hold as still as you comfortably can for 10 seconds.

C. Pointing accuracy (standard target test)

Move to the highlighted circle and press Space (or click) as fast and accurately as you can. It jumps across the ring of circles. 4 rounds of 13. With the ring, press Space with your other hand.

D. Timeline & knobs

Like editing in a DAW: land on the highlighted beat in the timeline or the highlighted small knob, then press Space. 24 targets.
